Nexum Tune
Vocal Pitch & Timing

01

See the voice as editable notes.

Import WAV, AIFF or FLAC, drop audio into the editor or record the input. Monophonic analysis displays notes, pitch curves and uncertain audible segments. Real silence remains empty; uncertain estimates stay visible and editable.

02

Intonation at a glance.

Note colours show the average distance from the nearest target in the selected scale: up to 10 cents, 10–25 cents or more than 25 cents. Grey marks uncertain detection, helping you decide where to listen more closely.

03

The tools above the timeline.

Select or lasso notes, change pitch, move timing, stretch length and adjust each segment’s gain. Split, join, erase and duplicate notes, with undo and redo. Pitch-centre correction supports a chosen key, scale and correction strength.

04

Less vibrato, when you want it.

Use the vibrato tool to reduce pitch movement within a selected note. Keep control of the correction amount rather than moving the entire note to a different pitch.

05

Copy a phrase into a harmony.

Duplicate one note or a multi-note selection and transpose the copies up or down. Originals and copies are rendered together and retained in the project. This creates layers from detected notes; it does not separate voices inside a recorded chord.

06

Listen to the exact passage.

Set the playhead from the upper ruler, draw a preview loop and compare the original with the edited recording. Import, record, play/pause, stop, host follow and loop controls live in the header, beside bypass, Master and L/R meters.

07

Capture in time with the host.

Record requests host playback and waits for the running timeline. If the DAW does not support automatic start, arm Tune and press Play in the host. Recording supports up to 60 seconds.

08

Return audio to your arrangement.

Wait for the offline render, export a WAV or drag the prepared audio into your DAW’s timeline. Original audio and edits are stored in the plugin state. The SPECTRUM page offers another view of the recording.

Back into the track

Export the edited recording or drag its WAV file into your timeline.

Designed primarily for monophonic vocals. Individual pitches inside recorded chords cannot currently be separated and edited independently. Pitch detection is an estimate, especially for breaths, noise and overlapping voices. Edits use offline rendering; Tune is not advertised as an ARA or live pitch-correction plugin.

Install in three steps.

  1. Extract

    Download the Windows ZIP and extract it into its own folder.

  2. Copy the VST3

    Copy the entire Nexum Tune.vst3 folder to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\NEXUM.

  3. Scan for plugins

    Run a fresh plugin scan in your DAW and load Nexum Tune as an audio effect. The EXE in the ZIP opens the standalone.
