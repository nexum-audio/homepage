Nexum
Hybrid synthesizer

01

Three layers. Five ways to start.

Stack OSC A, B and C in a horizontal layout with the waveform beside the controls. Choose Wavetable, Sample, Multisample, Granular or Spectral mode for each oscillator. Add up to 16 unison voices, detune, blend and warp.

02

Patch the sound before the filter.

Drag cables between generators, filters and destinations in MIX. Click a generator-to-filter connection to add its own FX rack. The rack appears as a dedicated FX tab, with a compact list of its effects beside the cable.

03

A signal path you can read.

Large IN and OUT sockets, thicker hanging cables and saved cable colours make routes easier to follow. Route to two independent filters, Bus 1, Bus 2, Main or Direct. Generator, filter, bus and output strips have distinct surfaces.

04

Modulation without the guesswork.

Four envelopes with BPM/MS timing, eight drawable LFOs with sync, retrigger and one-shot modes, plus velocity and note curves. The simplified MATRIX page brings live modulation and macro routes together, with editable ranges and removable assignments.

05

Macros that grow with the patch.

Start with four macros and add more when you need them. Save their names, colours, values and links in the preset. Use reversed or forward target ranges; the first eight macros also have stable host automation parameters.

06

Your own sound library.

Import wavetables, samples and SFZ multisamples from files, folders or ZIPs. Organise library entries with folders, names, categories, ratings, author, description, notes and tags. Move presets between packs with drag and drop.

07

A foundation, then a performance.

Six directly selectable sub shapes and 20 built-in noise sounds complement the oscillators. Use the arpeggiator, clip editor, pitch and modulation wheels, mono/legato modes and portamento controls to shape the performance.

08

One effects engine across the family.

Use the same 17 rack modules as Nexum FX, including Trancegate and Convolve. Save a single effect or a complete chain in the shared FX Library, then recall it in the synth or the separate insert plugin.

Add filters, the arpeggiator, clips and effects. Save the entire sound as a preset.

External sound packs are not included. External preset import is experimental: missing wavetables, samples and unsupported modulation or effect settings can change the result. Imported sounds are not guaranteed to match the source instrument.

Install in three steps.

  1. Extract

    Download the Windows ZIP and extract it into its own folder.

  2. Copy the VST3

    Copy the entire Nexum.vst3 folder to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\NEXUM.

  3. Scan for plugins

    Run a fresh plugin scan in your DAW and load Nexum as an instrument. The EXE in the ZIP opens the standalone.
