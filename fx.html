Nexum FX
Modular effect rack

01

Same effects. Same sound.

Nexum and Nexum FX share their effect implementations. New effects are built into both products from the same codebase.

02

One rack, your signal

Combine effects such as Distortion, Chorus, Delay, Convolve and Trancegate. Change their order and control bypass directly on each module.

03

One shared FX library

Save individual effects and complete chains. The library is shared with Nexum, including names, categories, tags and ratings.

04

Macros for your workflow

Link several controls to one macro and save assignments with your project. The first eight macros are also available for DAW automation.

Install in three steps.

  1. Extract

    Download the Windows ZIP and extract it into its own folder.

  2. Copy the VST3

    Copy the entire Nexum FX.vst3 folder to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\NEXUM.

  3. Scan for plugins

    Run a fresh plugin scan in your DAW and load Nexum FX as an audio effect. The EXE in the ZIP opens the standalone.
